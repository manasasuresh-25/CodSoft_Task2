## boxplot Explanation
The Box: Most items are from 2012 to 2019.
The Middle Line: The exact center year is 2016.
The Whiskers: Normal data spans from 2002 to 2023.
The Circles: Rare, old items from 1940 to 2001.
** Main Takeaway**
The dataset favors new entries over historical ones. Older years are flagged as anomalies because they are so rare.
